## Rules / traps to watch

Baseline list. Setup removes what cannot apply to me and adds what my situation creates.

- **Wash sale:** selling at a loss and buying the same/substantially identical security within 30 days before or after disallows the loss, including across accounts. If the repurchase is in an IRA, the loss is gone permanently.
- **IRAs are effectively cash accounts:** watch settlement (T+1) and good-faith violations. No short selling or naked options.
- **Short-term gains** (held ≤1 year) in a taxable account are taxed as ordinary income; long-term gains get lower rates, possibly 0% at low income.
- **Kiddie tax** can apply to investment income if I'm under 19, or a full-time student under 24, and supported by my parents.
- **Over-contributing** to an IRA triggers a 6% excise tax per year until fixed. Contributions are capped at earned income.
- **Roth IRA:** contributions can be withdrawn anytime; earnings are locked until 59½ and 5 years after the first contribution.
- Use specific-lot identification when selling in a taxable account.
